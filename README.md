The line:

> Monday: deploy stuck at 99%, inbox at 4,000 unread, and stand-up asked my status. I said: 'Did you try turning Monday off and on again?'

Three concrete Monday disasters (a deploy frozen at 99%, an inbox at 4,000 unread, a stand-up asking for status) landing on the classic IT-support punchline turned back on Monday itself.

python3 main.py
